# About Me

I'm a Senior AI/ML Engineer based in Barcelona with more than 10 years of experience building large-scale data platforms and distributed systems.

For most of my career, I specialized in Data Engineering, designing cloud-native architectures, analytics platforms, and data pipelines processing over 50 billion events every day. In 2025, I transitioned into AI/ML Engineering, where I now help build the infrastructure, tooling, and operational foundations that enable machine learning systems to move from experimentation to production.

Today my work focuses on MLOps, machine learning platforms, decision systems, and software engineering. As part of King's MLOps organization, I contribute to systems that enable personalized player experiences and support the full machine learning lifecycle—from data and feature pipelines to deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

Outside of work, I enjoy building web and mobile applications, experimenting with IoT automation, and exploring emerging AI technologies and developer tooling.
